# Aura Content Strategy

## Overview

This document defines the content structure, messaging, and communication strategy for the Aura portfolio.

Aura is designed to present Sylvia Kunaai's expertise as an AI Engineer and Software Engineer through a clear, professional, and project-driven narrative.

---

## Objectives

- Establish a strong professional brand.
- Showcase technical expertise and engineering capability.
- Highlight impactful AI and software engineering projects.
- Demonstrate problem-solving, product thinking, and continuous learning.
- Create a memorable experience for recruiters, hiring managers, and collaborators.

---

## Target Audience

The portfolio is intended for:

- Recruiters
- Hiring Managers
- Engineering Leaders
- AI and Software Engineering Teams
- Technical Founders
- Potential Clients and Collaborators

---

## Core Message

I design and build intelligent software that combines modern engineering practices with artificial intelligence to solve real-world problems.

---

## Content Architecture

The portfolio will include the following sections:

- Home
- About
- Featured Project — Phoenix AI Workspace
- Selected Projects
- Engineering Journey
- Technical Skills
- Certifications
- Resume
- Contact

---

## Content Principles

All content should be:

- Clear and concise
- Technically accurate
- Authentic
- Results-oriented
- Focused on impact rather than buzzwords

---

## Writing Style

The portfolio should maintain a professional and confident tone while remaining approachable.

Every project should answer:

- What problem was solved?
- Why was it important?
- Which technologies were used?
- What was the outcome?
- What was learned?

---

## Long-Term Vision

Aura will evolve beyond a portfolio into a professional platform that documents projects, technical insights, research, and continuous growth as an AI Engineer.
